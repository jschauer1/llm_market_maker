# Execution

This initial map selects the most useful execution questions. Each study is bounded to its recorded population and window; follow the original evidence before applying a result elsewhere.

- [Strike-ladder smoothing](../../tickets/study/answer/2026-08-29-smile-smoothing-ladder-flatness/STUDY.md) — Do liquid within-event strike ladders depart enough from an isotonic fit to trade after spreads?
- [Entry timing](../../tickets/study/answer/2026-08-30-entry-timing/STUDY.md) — Does later entry improve a fixed-side position, and what breaks when relative and absolute timestamps reverse order?
- [Parlay markup](../../tickets/study/answer/2026-08-30-parlay-markup/STUDY.md) — Are cross-game parlays marked above their legs, and is the measurable markup available in a tradeable fade product?
- [Accumulation decay](../../tickets/study/answer/2026-09-01-accumulation-decay-probe/STUDY.md) — Does a determined-but-unpublished tracking result leave enough executable price headroom to exploit?
- [Aggregation gap](../../tickets/study/answer/2026-09-01-aggregation-gap-probe/STUDY.md) — Do expectation identities survive support-completeness checks, executable basket pricing, fees, and carry?
- [Maker-mode fill simulation](../../tickets/study/investigation/2026-09-03-maker-mode-fill-simulation/STUDY.md) — Can resting one cent inside the bid, then crossing later, beat immediate crossing under print-level conservative fills? Pending.
- [Quoted prices versus executable evidence](../lessons/executable-prices-require-tradeable-quotes.md) — Which recorded quote fields must survive collection before a historical edge can be called fillable?
