# FDA obstacle judgment versus explicit disclosure

**Date:** 2026-09-05 · **Tier:** pending input/provenance audit · **Verdict:** Investigation in progress.

## Question and approved scope

Does price-blind semantic interpretation of dated, application-specific FDA
obstacles identify defensible signals beyond a deterministic disclosure
baseline, and is the available evidence sufficient to test incremental net
return? This is the finite FDA experiment approved after the viability review.
It creates study evidence, not production probabilities, bets or a replacement
for legislation v1. No theory lifecycle changes are included.

## Frozen population and exposure rules

Primary families: KXFDAAPPROVE, KXFDAAPPROVALDATECMPS,
KXFDAAPPROVALDATENTLA, KXFDARETATRUTIDE and KXFDAVERVE102. Include only
marketing-approval-by-deadline rules for a named drug and indication. Exclude
FDADECISION, submission, rulemaking and generic approval-count contracts;
their payout conditions are different. Union current/events and archived
partitions by ticker. Count one drug/application subject across its date rungs.
Use actual stated deadlines, not realized close times or expected expiration.

Historical diagnostic: for each subject, take its earliest stated-deadline
contract within 1–365 days of opening; decide at 12:00 UTC on the first full
UTC calendar day after that contract opens. Keep every subject and source,
quote or horizon exclusion. Previously read outcomes and source cases remain
development/exposed; a fresh judge does not turn them into validation.
Do not inspect the old legislation holdout.

Current diagnostic: one nearest future deadline within 365 days per pending
subject, using the saved September 5 board and fresh targeted status/rules.
Exclude already-approved subjects. Freeze source eligibility at
2026-09-05T18:30:00Z; later collection may retrieve earlier published original
documents but must document that distinction. These decisions can subsequently
be scored; no future outcome is assumed. Any subject used in historical
development remains development at later deadlines. Only distinct, previously
unresolved subjects can supply prospective outcome validation. Cases already
researched to design the prompt are marked exposed even while unresolved.

## Source contract

Both procedures receive identical source text. Per case retrieve the latest
SEC 10-Q/10-K available by the decision, the latest three sponsor releases
before that decision whose title/body concerns the named drug and FDA,
regulatory review, filing, trial results or manufacturing, and the latest
available FDA application-specific document. Preserve missing categories,
failed requests and the search/query receipt. Include relevant FDA pathway
guidance for possible exceptions. Reuse saved originals when applicable.

Retain complete original bytes, publication/filing and retrieval times, URLs
and hashes. The compact judge pack contains complete pertinent regulatory,
clinical-readiness and manufacturing paragraphs with adjacent qualifications,
including adverse and favorable routes; a collector must not write a verdict
or a paraphrased forecast. Excerpts identify source and location. Missing
date/vintage or inadequate coverage remains a flagged limitation, never
silently treated as a contemporaneous packet. Current-page metadata alone
cannot prove historical availability of mutable content.

## Comparison fixed before judgments

Primary baseline: deterministic extraction of an affirmative statement that
a required filing/resubmission is planned after the market deadline, or an
explicit inability to obtain approval by that deadline. Parse stated calendar
quarters, halves, months and dates conservatively: the earliest point in the
stated window must be at or after the exclusive deadline. Unknown dates,
generic risk language, negated plans and hypothetical boilerplate do not
signal. Keep exact matched text and parsing failures. A before-deadline PDUFA
goal, an adverse advisory vote or merely having a CRL never suffices.

Secondary warning-only control: explicit expected-CRL or current
manufacturing-deficiency language, without the primary timeline requirement.
This measures the value of the cheap warning rule and its false positives;
it must not be presented as the sole benchmark if the stronger baseline wins.

Semantic procedure: independent Sol/high, web search disabled, with its
documented February 16, 2026 cutoff verified from official provider material.
Record requested identity and any unresolved runtime snapshot honestly. Its
rendered prompt and bare source payload are saved through tools.judgments
before dispatch. It sees exact rules and dated sources, but no prices,
outcomes, baseline selections, preferred direction or research conclusions.
Labels: substantive_barrier, plausible_path, formalities_only,
insufficient_evidence. Require source-cited necessary step, documented
obstacle/timing conflict, applicability and bypass assessment. Pending,
silence, standard review durations and nonbinding advisory votes alone do not
establish a barrier. Only substantive_barrier is a hypothetical NO selection.
Missing affirmative route evidence cannot be relabeled plausible_path.

Freeze code/prompt and batch inputs before computing their comparisons.
Retain all first outputs, including format or reasoning failures. No tuning
and rerunning the same cases as confirmation. Compare every disagreement
with original sources; report whether it is an additional supported signal,
a correctly avoided false positive, an unsupported claim or an abstention.
Source correctness review remains distinct from eventual outcome correctness.

## Entry, economics and inference limits

Historical entry is the first valid bid/ask candle at or after decision plus
15 minutes, within the following 60 minutes; use one minus the YES bid for
NO. Require both sides in (0,1), spread at most 0.07 and decision preceding
settlement. Do not select earlier attractive trade prints. Missing historical
depth limits the result to displayed-price sensitivity, with no proven size.
Fee history and rule vintages are audited; unsupported inputs cannot qualify.

For current cases capture books immediately after first judgments are saved,
for both selected and unselected subjects. These are delayed observation
quotes, not as-of historical entries. Record actual latency and 100/500/1000
contract depth curves, adverse price movement and dated series taker fees.
No introspected probability, expected-profit forecast or synthetic fill is
recorded as an observation.

Report label counts, source-supported disagreements, baseline overlap,
abstentions and the retrospective per-subject after-fee return sensitivity.
Compare both signal-only returns and returns per admitted subject with
abstentions assigned zero exposure. Repeated rungs do not enlarge n.

The prior census has only a handful of resolved subjects, already inspected.
It cannot independently validate a 3-cent edge. Before judging, compute a
planning MDE sensitivity `2.8 * sigma / sqrt(n)` at sigma 0.25 and 0.50 and
show the independent-subject ceiling; this is approximate planning, not an
inferential result. If usable untouched history cannot support the target,
run only the explicitly diagnostic comparison and freeze any genuinely new
subject decisions for later scoring. Do not reinterpret a small diagnostic
as a powered return test or impose a live-only restriction on future valid
historical evidence.

Continue semantic development only if it supplies source-supported information
beyond the primary baseline or demonstrably handles a material exception the
baseline misses. If it merely repeats explicit disclosures, retain the useful
mechanical signal and stop expanding the semantic layer on this evidence.
Unsupported barriers or treating advisory/calendar warnings as binding are
procedure failures. A definitive profitability claim requires independent
net-return and incremental-return evidence after fees, slippage and research
cost; a powered nonpositive result would reject that investment case.

## Preservation and scope

Study files are the only new implementation owner. Reuse shared receipt and

outputs and perishable sources persist in batches. Commit this protocol before
