## Frozen census and prospective discovery

Use one complete shared open-board snapshot. Every returned market enters
the cheap screen; every exclusion has a reason. Exclude closed markets,
missing/invalid two-sided asks, spread above 12 cents, open interest below 100,
or scheduled close outside 6 hours–365 days. Price filters are symmetric:
no favorite-side or FDA/bill allowlist. Scheduled close is only a scheduling
proxy; use actual payout deadlines for judgment and effect measurement.

Exclude clearly mechanical price, weather, live sporting performance and
aggregate numeric measurement contracts by explicit category/mechanics rules.
Retain institutional actions within those categories (player transfers,
product releases, agency decisions). Unknown mechanics pass. Group by event
for efficient reading but retain every admitted contract/deadline/subject;
event grouping does not assert independence. Preserve gate removals by
category and a deterministic audit sample of removed contracts.

An independent affordable judge (requested `gpt-5.6-sol`, high effort; alias
unresolved) researches survivors in stable hash order. Input contains exact
title/rules and decision date, never prices, volumes, outcomes, baseline
selection or previous verdicts. Classify each contract:
`substantive_barrier`, `plausible_path`, `formalities_only`,
`insufficient_evidence`, or `not_applicable`. Barrier requires a documented
necessary unfinished step and current obstacle; silence is not an obstacle.
Plausible path requires affirmative support. Formalities means the substantive
decision is completed and only a routine final act remains. Missing/stale
status produces insufficient evidence. Retain dated sources, short quotes,
mandatory steps and independent-subject keys. Current sources may be searched;
historical judgment may not use current web.

Save first outputs, exact prompts, input hashes and actual settings in
incremental batches. Requote after judgment using actual books/series fees;
preserve missingness and time lag. Report payable YES/NO prices and depth.
Never ask the judge for probabilities, prices or edge. Paid work survives
interruption.
