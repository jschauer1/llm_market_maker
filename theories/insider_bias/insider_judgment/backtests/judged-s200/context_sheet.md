# Background mechanism sheet — judged-s200 backtest

Purpose: backtest judges run with web search OFF (tier B requirement), so
this sheet supplies the *mechanism-level* background a live judge could
have looked up. Rules of construction, for auditors: notes are written at
the series-family level and apply uniformly to every event in that family;
they describe **how resolution works structurally** (live vs pre-taped,
who decides, scripted vs spontaneous), never specific events, outcomes,
prices, or anything dated after January 2026. If a note and the market's
own rules text conflict, trust the rules text.

## Award shows and prizes
(KXESPYS, KXBET, KXEMMYNOMS, KXEMMYDSACTO, KXEMMYLIMITEDACTO, KXFIELDS)
- Major award winners are usually determined by ballots that close days to
  weeks before the ceremony; a small tabulation and production group knows
  results before broadcast. Ceremonies air live, but the outcome is
  typically already decided and sealed.
- Nomination lists (e.g. Emmy nominations) are tabulated before the
  announcement; the academy and its accountants know the list in advance.
- Some fan-voted award categories keep voting open until near or during
  the show; check the market rules for which kind of category this is.
- The Fields Medal is decided by a small IMU committee long before the
  public announcement at the ICM.

## Music charts and streaming metrics
(KXTOPSONG, KXRANKLIST1SONG, KXBBCHARTPOSITIONSONG, KX20SONG, KXAISONG,
KXSPOTIFYNUM1USA, KXKXSPOTIFYNUM1GLB, KXMOSTSTREAMED, KXALBUMDEBUT,
KXALBUMSALES, KXALBUMVS, KXMLABELSHARE, KXFOLLOWERCOUNTCLAV)
- Billboard charts aggregate a full tracking week (Fri–Thu) and publish
  days later; the outcome is the behavior of millions of listeners, not a
  single group's decision. Industry mid-week tracking projections
  circulate among labels and chart-watchers, so insiders can *forecast*
  late in the week, but nobody decides the number.
- Spotify daily charts and follower counts are published aggregates of
  consumer behavior; the platform sees them first but they are not a
  decision anyone has made.
- Label market-share trackers aggregate many releases' performance.

## Reality and competition television
(KXLIUSAELIMINATIONW, KXLOVEISLANDUKRANK, KXBIGBROTHERELIMINATION,
KXCHOPPEDCASTAWAYS)
- Big Brother (US): eviction votes are cast live on the broadcast;
  the outcome is genuinely undecided until airtime, although competition
  segments and house events are recorded ahead of the episode and known
  to production. Check whether the market's window resolves on a live
  vote or on already-taped house events.
- Love Island (US/UK): airs near-live (roughly a day behind the villa);
  eliminations often depend on public app votes taken shortly before an
  episode, but production and the villa know intermediate states (who is
  vulnerable, couplings) ahead of broadcast. Final rankings combine a
  public vote revealed live.
- Chopped and similar cooking competitions are fully pre-taped, often
  months in advance; cast, crew, and production know the winner long
  before air.

## Releases, products, and corporate decisions
(KXSONGRELEASEDATE, KXALBUMRELEASEDATE, KXALBUMLENGTH, KXFEATURE,
KXFEATURETRIPPIERED, KXGPT, KXGROK, KXGTAPREORDERS, KXMC,
KXCOMPANYLAYOFF, KXFDAAPPROVE, KXFDAANNOUNCE, KXCRITICALITY)
- Release dates, tracklists, features, and album lengths are known to the
  artist's team and label in advance, and often to press and supply
  chain; leaks are common. Plans do change late, so knowledge is real but
  not immutable.
- AI-lab model releases are known internally ahead of time, but timing
  slips routinely; version naming is a marketing decision made late.
- Metacritic scores aggregate many critics' reviews published under
  embargo; no single group knows the final average in advance.
- FDA approvals and reclassifications follow internal review timelines
  known to the agency and the sponsor company (PDUFA-style target dates
  are public); the decision itself is made before it is announced.
- Nuclear plant criticality milestones are scheduled by the operator and
  regulator; the companies involved know their own timelines.
- Corporate layoffs are decided by leadership before any announcement.

## AI leaderboards and usage rankings
(KXTECHRANKLISTAICODE, KXLLM1, KXTOPUSAGEAI, KXARENASCORE, KXANTHSHARE,
KXBABASHARE, KXGOOGSHARE, KXFRONTIER)
- Arena-style leaderboards (crowd-voted head-to-head) and usage/market-
  share trackers aggregate many independent users; labs know their own
  release plans but cannot know a crowd score before it accumulates.
- "Best/top model this week/month" markets resolve on the published
  state of such aggregators at a deadline.

## Individual-behavior and count markets
(KXTRUMPTIME, KXTRUMPPHOTO, KXLAGODAYS, KXTRUMPMEET, KXDJTATTACKWARSH,
KXMAMDANIEO, KXTRUMPNOMNUM, KXISRNORMCOUNT)
- Markets on one person's routine behavior (posting windows, trips,
  photographed days, public criticism) resolve on what that person
  happens to do; the person and close staff have some foreknowledge of
  schedules, but much of it is genuinely spontaneous.
- Executive orders, nominations sent to the Senate, and diplomatic
  agreements are decisions a small group (an administration) makes and
  schedules before announcing.

## Legislative, confirmation, and court markets
(KXBLANCHECOUNT, KXBLANCHEJUDICIARY, KXBLANCHEWITHDRAW, KXCLAYTONCOUNT,
KXCLAYTONDNI, KXCDCCONF, KXADJOURNRECESS, KXHBUDGETRES, KXHFHOUSING,
KXFETTERMANSWITCH, KXBURNHAMJUL, KXBOLOJANOUT, KX14AMENDCASE)
- Floor and committee schedules are set by leadership; whip counts give
  leadership a good, not perfect, advance picture of vote outcomes.
  Exact roll-call totals are less knowable than pass/fail.
- Withdrawal of a nomination is a decision the administration and nominee
  make before it is public.
- Appellate and Supreme Court outcomes are decided in conference well
  before the opinion publishes; only the justices and clerks know.
- A politician's party switch or a foreign PM's resignation is a personal
  decision known first to a small circle.

## "Reported by source agencies" markets
(KXBALLROOMHALT, KXCRUDEEXPORTBAN, KXFABLERESTORE, KXEBOLACOUNTRY,
KXJAPANFOODTAX, KXANTIWEPTAX, KXBIDENTAPES, KXEPSTEIN, KXAGENCIES,
KXBEEFTARIFF)
- These resolve when named media/agencies report a thing; the actor
  (a government, a company) knows before any report exists. Reporting
  lag can matter: an event near the close may happen but not be
  reported in time. Check the rules for which sources count.

## Auctions and sales
(KXART)
- Live auction hammer prices are determined by bidding in the room at
  sale time. Auction houses know reserves, estimates, and registered
  interest beforehand — informative, but the final price is not decided
  in advance by anyone.

## Fundraising and finance disclosures
(KXACTBLUETOP, KXDNCCASH)
- These resolve on published dashboards or filings; the organization
  sees its own numbers continuously before the public snapshot.

## Live performances and broadcasts
(KXWCFIRSTSONG)
- Setlists and opening/halftime performances are rehearsed and known to
  production, performers, and crew before the broadcast.
