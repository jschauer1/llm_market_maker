# Separate selected contrasts from fresh mechanism tests

**Summary:** Label the best split as selected, use a split-free statistic for description, and demand fresh data for the mechanism claim.
**Applies to:** Ordered-bin analyses where a breakpoint or long-versus-short contrast is chosen after inspecting results.
**Finding:** Measured (2026-08-29 and 2026-09-01): the reported politics contrast was the best of three splits and looked like one level shift, not a gradient; the long-horizon direction later reversed in the fresh econfin population.
**Do next time:** Show all candidate splits, quote the choice-free estimator, avoid mechanism language stronger than the shape, and preregister the replication population.
**Evidence:** [gradient review — selected maximum](../studies/answer/2026-08-29-calibration-harvest-gradient-review/STUDY.md#3-the-reported-contrast-was-not-pre-registered-and-its-t-is-a-maximum); [gradient review — split-free statistic](../studies/answer/2026-08-29-calibration-harvest-gradient-review/STUDY.md#4-the-split-free-statistic-which-is-what-should-be-quoted); [RESULTS.md — horizon reversal](../RESULTS.md#the-horizon-axis-reverses-sign-out-of-sample)
