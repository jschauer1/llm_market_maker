# Let full coverage settle the claim, then mine separately

**Summary:** Walk the complete scoped population, then treat any post-mortem survivor as a separate hypothesis with no inherited record.
**Applies to:** Mechanical backtests where the API-reachable population is finite and affordable to enumerate.
**Finding:** Measured (2026-08-25): full coverage of the sample's window removed the mention-family edge; a post-hoc NO-favorite subset later founded `no_side_premium`, but the exploratory rows did not transfer as its track record.
**Do next time:** Label samples unconfirmed, run full coverage when feasible, mine the failure transparently, and preregister survivors under their own theory or slice identity.
